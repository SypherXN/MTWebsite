## Process

We identified Up The River's core: pressure to keep moving, special help/hinder elements, and a changing board. Ideas ranged from circular chase boards to reversing tracks; we chose player-built rails destroyed from behind.

**Iteration 1.** Separate rail lines, five-card hands, draw on play, forward movement each turn, trailing tiles destroyed. Cards need dice checks; sabotage includes dynamite and stalactite. Original goal: reach a finish line.

**Iteration 2.** Playtests showed early eliminations, weak panic, and unfair cards. We added starting rails, changed the win condition to last player standing, and introduced the rocket to counter stalactite. Tuned success rates and interactions across the deck.
