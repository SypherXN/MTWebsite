## Duties

- Cooperated with 9 other students to produce a game designed around a mind control mechanic
- Led development on enemy and ally unit AI
- Utilized GitHub for version control to streamline code organization
- Synchronized with other team members during daily Scrum meetings
- Developed a way to convert enemy units into ally units
- Created a unit skill system to allow unique combat and puzzles

## Gallery

Screenshots and in-engine captures from development are included in the project teaser linked in the hero video.

## Process

The team scoped the entire experience around mind control as the core fantasy. My work centered on AI that could serve as both threat and tool: conversion from enemy to ally, skill-driven combat behaviors, and puzzle interactions that depended on which unit the player controlled at a given moment.
